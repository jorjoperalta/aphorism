3-Day PPL Hypertrophy Program

Optimized Reference Dashboard

🔴 Day 1: Push ~53–60 min
Chest, Shoulders & Triceps
1. Barbell Bench Press 3 sets × 6–8
⏱️ Rest: 3 min 🏋️ Heavy Compound
Touch mid-chest, drive feet down into the floor.
2. Standing Overhead Press 3 sets × 6–8
⏱️ Rest: 3 min 🏋️ Heavy Compound
Keep glutes squeezed tight to lock core and protect lumbar.
3. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets × 8–10
⏱️ Rest: 2.5 min 🏋️ Mod. Compound
Set bench to 30° angle to minimize front delt takeover.
4. Cable / DB Chest Flys 3 sets × 10–12
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Deep stretch at bottom; squeeze peak contraction at top.
5. Lateral Raises 3 sets × 12–15
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Lean forward slightly, lead with elbows, control negative.
6. Tricep Rope Pushdowns 3 sets × 10–12
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Keep upper arms pinned firmly to sides throughout set.
🟢 Day 2: Pull ~43–50 min
Back, Biceps & Rear Delts
1. Lat Pulldowns 3 sets × 8–10
⏱️ Rest: 3 min 🏋️ Heavy Compound
Pull to upper chest with slight backward lean for lats.
2. Seated Cable Rows 3 sets × 8–10
⏱️ Rest: 2.5 min 🏋️ Mod. Compound
Mid-back focus. Squeeze shoulder blades fully at peak.
3. Face Pulls 3 sets × 12–15
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Rear delts & rotator cuff. Pull rope toward eyes/forehead.
4. Bicep Curls (DB or BB) 3 sets × 10–12
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Strict posture—avoid torso swinging to keep tension on biceps.
5. Farmer's Walk 3 sets × 45–60s
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Core & Grip
Walk tall with heavy dumbbells, shoulders pinned back.
🔵 Day 3: Legs ~43–50 min
Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes & Calves
1. Hack Squat / Barbell Squat 3 sets × 6–8
⏱️ Rest: 3 min 🏋️ Heavy Compound
Primary quad driver. Lower controlled until thighs hit parallel.
2. Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs) 3 sets × 8–10
⏱️ Rest: 3 min 🏋️ Heavy Compound
Primary hamstring hinge. Hinge at hips, push glutes far back.
3. Leg Extensions 3 sets × 10–12
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Pause 1 second at top extension for peak quad contraction.
4. Seated or Lying Leg Curls 3 sets × 10–12
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Knee flexion focus. Flex toes toward shins to keep tension.
5. Hip Adductor / Abductor 3 sets × 12–15
⏱️ Rest: 2 min ⚡ Isolation
Inner thigh & outer glute machine superset.
